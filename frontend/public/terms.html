What the app is for

LionWeather shows weather readings and forecasts for general information. It is not an official warning service. For weather warnings, flood alerts and haze advisories, follow NEA and PUB directly.

Where the data comes from

Readings and forecasts come from NEA through data.gov.sg and from Open-Meteo. The rain forecast model is our own and is often wrong. Readings can be delayed, missing or inaccurate, and the app can be unavailable without notice.
