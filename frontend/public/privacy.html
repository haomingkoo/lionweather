Privacy

Last updated 25 October 2026

LionWeather is a free Singapore weather dashboard. It is run by Koo Haoming (individual, Singapore). This page explains what the app does with your data. It is written to the standard of Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA).

There are no accounts, sign-ups or cookies. The main personal data the app handles is your location and the address of your device on the internet (your IP address).

Your location

When you press Use my location, your browser asks for permission. If you allow it, the app reads your GPS coordinates and uses them only to show weather for where you are. The coordinates go to:

  • Our server (hosted on Railway). It looks up the nearest weather station and forecast area. The app does not write your coordinates to its database. Request logging is switched off on our server.
  • Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com). Our server asks it for weather at your coordinates when no nearby station reading is available. Open-Meteo sees our server's address, not yours.
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim (nominatim.openstreetmap.org). Our server asks it for the name of your area. If that fails, your browser asks Nominatim directly. In that case Nominatim also sees your IP address.

Live location. Once you have added your location, the app re-reads your GPS every 15 minutes while the tab is open, and again on each visit. Each reading is sent as described above. You can turn this off with the Live location button under the search box. Deleting your location card also turns it off. Your browser's site settings can revoke location permission completely.

Places and postal codes you search for

When you type a place name or postal code, your browser sends that text directly to OneMap (onemap.gov.sg, run by the Singapore Land Authority) and to OpenStreetMap Nominatim. Both see your IP address and what you typed. A postal code can be a home address, so search for a nearby landmark if you prefer.

Stored on your device

The app saves these items in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device unless noted above.

Rain alerts are notifications shown by your own browser. We do not collect an email address or phone number. To remove everything, delete your location cards or clear this site's data in your browser.

How long data is kept

  • Your coordinates and searches are not stored by our app after each request finishes.
  • Rate-limit entries are held in memory for about a minute.
  • Railway and Cloudflare keep their own operational logs under their retention policies. [LAWYER REVIEW: confirm provider log retention periods]
  • Local storage stays on your device until you delete it.

Your rights

Under the PDPA you can ask what personal data we hold about you and how it has been used. You can ask us to correct it. You can also withdraw consent at any time. To withdraw consent for location, turn off live location, delete your location card and revoke the permission in your browser. Because the app does not keep your data on its server, most requests can be handled on your own device. If you still have a question, email us and we will reply within 30 days. [LAWYER REVIEW]

Children

The app is a general weather tool and is not aimed at children.
