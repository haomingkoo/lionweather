Privacy

Last updated 26 October 2026

LionWeather is a free Singapore weather app run by Koo Haoming (individual, Singapore). There are no accounts and no cookies.

What we collect

  • Your GPS coordinates, if you press Use my location and your browser allows it.
  • Place names or postal codes you type into the search box.
  • Your IP address, which every website sees when you load it.

Why

We use your location only to show weather for where you are. We use your IP address to stop one device from flooding the server with requests.

Who sees it

  • Our server on Railway gets your coordinates to find the nearest weather station. It does not save them, and its request logging is off.
  • Open-Meteo gets your coordinates from our server when no nearby station has data. It never sees your IP address.
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim turns coordinates and searches into place names. Sometimes your browser asks it directly, so it also sees your IP address.
  • OneMap (Singapore Land Authority) gets the postal codes you search for, straight from your browser.
  • OpenStreetMap tile servers send the map images, so they see your IP address.
  • Cloudflare delivers the site and blocks abuse.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics: cookieless, aggregate page views, no input capture.

Railway, Cloudflare and OpenStreetMap run servers outside Singapore, so your data may be handled overseas. Each provider has its own privacy policy.

How long

  • Our server forgets your coordinates once it has answered.
  • It keeps your IP address in memory for about a minute for rate limiting.
  • Railway and Cloudflare keep their own request logs under their own policies.
  • Your saved locations and settings stay in your browser until you delete them.

Your choices

  • Once you add your location, the app re-reads your GPS every 15 minutes while the tab is open. Use the Following my location button to turn that off.
  • Delete a location card to remove it. Deleting your GPS card also turns off live location.
  • Clear this site's data in your browser to remove everything it stored. That is four local storage keys:
    • lionweather_locations: your location cards and their last weather
    • geolocation_permission: whether live location is on
    • lionweather_rain_notify: whether rain alerts are on
    • lionweather_rain_notify_last: when the last rain alert fired
  • The light and dark theme follows Singapore's sunrise and sunset. It is worked out in your browser and not stored.
  • Rain alerts are notifications from your own browser. We never ask for your email or phone number.
  • You can ask what data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or withdraw consent. Email us and we will reply within 30 days.
